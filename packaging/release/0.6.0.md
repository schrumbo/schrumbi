- **KI as a tool, not a feature.** If Claude Code, Codex or OpenCode is installed, a quiet KI column next to your pages can read your semester, create cards, file inbox items and set things up. Every change is shown as a diff first and undoes in one step. Shell stays off unless you allow it.
- **No setup wizard.** The overview shows a short setup list you can hide, and a tip of the day points at the feature it explains.
- **Calendar beyond the week.** Week and month views, paging with ← and →, breaks shaded in both.
- **Every open is a tab.** No more preview tabs; the sidebar stays a slim icon rail.
- **Drag PDFs and pages.** Drop a PDF or a single page into a note to embed it, or a page from one PDF into another in split view.
- **Inbox filing with KI.** Module, kind, title, date and due date are suggested in the table; you check and confirm.
- **Narrow windows.** Works down to 640 px wide, so a 50/50 split fits on a laptop.
