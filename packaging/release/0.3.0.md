- **Office files in Schrumbi.** Word and PowerPoint open as pages; Excel, ODS and old .xls files open as a sheet with tabs, frozen panes, formats, selection, copy, search and zoom. Broken and password-protected files say why.
- **Add for a day.** Right-click a day on the overview, or its +, to add a submission, task or exam due that day.
- **Apple Calendar (macOS).** Deadlines and the timetable stay in a calendar called „Schrumbi“, kept up to date as you work.
- **Smooth reading mode.** Long notes, lists and tables scroll and select without lag.
- **No automatic renames.** Name by convention… in the ⋯ menu, the tree, the file list and the palette shows an editable suggestion first.
- **PDF export asks before replacing** an earlier export, with Don't ask again and Undo.
- **Module page in one view:** every deadline with its files, notes, cards, sessions, grade and links.
- **Submissions count linked files** and week folders instead of guessing by date.
- **Safer dialogs.** Enter clicks the focused button, and delete dialogs start on Cancel.
